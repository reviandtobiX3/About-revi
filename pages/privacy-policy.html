Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how reviandtobi collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the portfolio website and associated project pages, including the Dress and Appearance product page.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require account creation, sign-in, or personal profile registration to browse the site. The website is a static portfolio and product showcase. We do not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or payment data through the site.

2. Use of Information

Any information you voluntarily provide through contact links, email, or feedback channels is used only to respond to your inquiry, review project feedback, or improve product communication.

3. Cookies and Analytics

This site does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies for behavioral tracking.

4. Third-Party Links

The site may contain links to external resources, product pages, contact destinations, and social media platforms such as Twitch, TikTok, YouTube, and Discord. Those third-party sites are governed by their own privacy terms and policies, which are separate from this site. We do not control, own, or operate those platforms, and any information collected by them is subject to their own privacy practices.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the integrity of the site and its content. Because this is a static site, the overall data footprint remains minimal.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in site structure or legal requirements. Any updates will be reflected in the current document date shown above.
